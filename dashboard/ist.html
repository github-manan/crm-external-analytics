What's replicated vs. redefined
  • KPI tiles compare to the previous week here, not "last year" — the source dashboard's comparison basis wasn't consistent from tile to tile.
  • Deal Movement is split by pipeline here (Consulting/Datasurfr/MSS/Renewal each have different stages) — the source dashboard blends all four into one axis, which the README explicitly warns against.
  • Started as a copy of the native Zoho "IST Dashboard", then merged with the Weekly Review: every section follows the Week picker.
  • For a past week, deals are shown as they stood that Sunday, rebuilt from deal history. Owner and pipeline are today's values, and closing dates can be slightly off (Zoho only logs them on a stage change).
  • Lead status and Hot Leads have no history in Zoho: for a past week they cover leads that existed by then, but with today's status and score.
  • Zoho's own "Last 3 Months" table is actually 3 weeks by its date ranges — ours is honestly labeled as weeks.
